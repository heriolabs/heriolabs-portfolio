My process

Practical thinking before automation.

I start by understanding the process, then choose the simplest useful workflow for the job.

01 / DISCOVERY

Understand

I clarify the goal, current process, and problem to solve.

02 / PLAN

Map the workflow

I agree on steps, tools, limitations, and human handoff points.

03 / BUILD

Configure & test

I test normal cases, exceptions, and missing information.

04 / HANDOVER

Explain clearly

I explain what is ready and how to use the workflow.
